# Agents Sleep Preventer 4.4.0

## Dictation

- **Dictation history**: every dictated message is saved to a timestamped history (last 500 entries), viewable via ••• → Dictation History.
- **Never lose a dictation again**: when the transcribed text lands nowhere (no field had keyboard focus, or insertion failed), a small pill appears at the bottom of the screen with the text, a Copy button and a 10-second countdown before it disappears. The text stays in the history either way.
