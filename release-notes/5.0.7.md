# Agents Sleep Preventer 5.0.7

## Dictation

- **Vocabulary words catch more mishearings.** Besides a swapped vowel ("Climo" for "Cleemo"), a similar-sounding consonant ("Clibo") or one extra consonant ("Climbo") is now corrected too, for words of five sounds or more. Real words that sound further away, like "climat", are left alone so everyday sentences are never changed.
