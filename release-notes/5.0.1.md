# Agents Sleep Preventer 5.0.1

## macOS dictation

- A full-screen gradient halo replaces the thin recording bar and reacts to your voice as you speak.
- Smooth entrance and rounded, feathered edges make recording status easier to see. Transcription uses a calmer, warmer animation.
- The halo respects Reduce Motion, stays transparent to clicks, and does not take keyboard focus.
- Live transcription previews remain above the halo, including on displays with an offset desktop position.

## Downloads

- macOS: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.0.1.dmg`, signed and notarized, with Sparkle updates.
- Windows x64: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.0.1-windows-x86_64.zip`. Windows behavior is unchanged; extract the full ZIP and launch `asp.exe`.
