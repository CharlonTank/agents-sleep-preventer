# Agents Sleep Preventer 4.2.0

## New: Streaming dictation with Parakeet v3

- **New dictation engine — Parakeet v3** (670 MB): near-instant transcription, English + 24 European languages with automatic language detection. Pick it in Settings → Dictation. Whisper Turbo remains available for best accuracy, 99 languages, and custom vocabulary.
- **Live transcription preview**: with Parakeet selected, your words appear in a pill at the bottom of the screen *while you speak* — release the hotkey and the final text is inserted instantly.
- **Customizable dictation hotkey**: choose between Fn+Shift (default), Fn+Control, Fn+Option, Control+Option, or Command+Shift in Settings → Dictation.

## New: Agent notifications

- Get a macOS notification when a coding agent **finishes a long task** (45s+) or **needs your attention** (waiting for input or a permission).
- **Click the notification to jump straight to that agent's terminal.**
- Toggle in Settings → Sleep Preventer → Agent Notifications.

## Simpler permissions setup

- Only **two permissions** now: Microphone and Accessibility (Input Monitoring is no longer needed).
- Clicking "Allow" on Accessibility now adds the app to System Settings automatically — just flip the switch, no more hunting with the "+" button.
- The app detects when it runs from outside /Applications (which silently breaks macOS permissions) and offers to move itself and relaunch.
- If macOS ever revokes a permission while the app runs, you get an alert with a shortcut to fix it — and dictation restarts on its own once re-granted.

## Fixes & cleanup

- Removed an unused bundled binary (smaller download).
- Onboarding and dialogs updated to reflect the configured hotkey and selected engine.
