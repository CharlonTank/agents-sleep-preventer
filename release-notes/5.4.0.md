# Agents Sleep Preventer 5.4.0

## New

- **Nightly sleep (macOS).** Pick a time in the **…** menu (**Nightly Sleep**: 22:00 to 03:00, or any time with **Custom…**). Every night from then on, the Mac goes to sleep the first time nobody is using it, even with agents still working and even when forced awake. Agents can't keep it awake again until you come back. Unlike the "Sleep when done" time limit, it stays on night after night.
  - It waits while you are at the Mac or watching a video.
  - A night the Mac already slept through is left alone: opening it in the morning won't put it back to sleep at your first coffee break.
  - From the terminal: `asp bedtime 2:00`, `asp bedtime off`.
- **Sleep on low battery (macOS), on by default at 10%.** On battery at or below that charge, agents no longer keep the Mac awake, and it goes to sleep as soon as nobody is using it instead of draining to zero. The menu shows **LOW BATTERY**. Change the threshold (5 to 20%) or turn it off in the **…** menu, or with `asp low-battery 15` / `asp low-battery off`.

## Downloads

- macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.4.0.dmg`, signed and notarized, with Sparkle updates, or `brew install --cask charlontank/tap/agents-sleep-preventer`.
- Windows x64: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.4.0-windows-x86_64.zip`. Extract the full ZIP and launch `asp.exe`.
