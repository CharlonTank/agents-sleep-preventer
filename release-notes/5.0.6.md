# Agents Sleep Preventer 5.0.6

## Dictation

- **Vocabulary words now work with every model, Parakeet included.** They used to be passed only to Whisper, so a word you added (say "Cleemo") could still come out as it sounds ("Climo"). Words that sound like one of your vocabulary words are now replaced by your spelling, including names split in two ("Clee mo") and at the start of a sentence. The matching is strict so ordinary words are left alone.
- The live preview shows the corrected spelling too.

## Downloads

- macOS: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.0.6.dmg`, signed and notarized, with Sparkle updates.
- Windows x64: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.0.6-windows-x86_64.zip`. Vocabulary words are applied the same way on Windows; extract the full ZIP and launch `asp.exe`.
