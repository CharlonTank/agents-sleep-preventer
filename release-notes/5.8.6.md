# Agent Session Pilot 5.8.6

## Telegram

- **Quiet nights.** From 23:00 to 08:00 on the machine's clock, what goes to the owner in private makes no sound: session messages, files, the bot's notices and the RAM and disk alerts. The owner reads them on waking. Project groups ring as by day, since their other members may live in another time zone. A session the owner wrote to in the last 30 minutes still rings: they're awake for it.
- `asp telegram send` then reminds the session that the owner sleeps: no questions at night, do what it would propose and say so in the morning. `--urgent` rings anyway, for what can't wait and only the owner can handle.
- `asp telegram morning` (or `matin`) waits for the end of the night, in the background, so a session can write once in the morning: what it did during the night, then its questions.
- The hours are `"night": ["23:00", "08:00"]` in the bot's `bot.json`; an empty list turns the night off. It is the same setting and the same journal field as sync-telegram's, so both tools agree.

## Downloads

- macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.8.6.dmg`, signed and notarized, with Sparkle updates, or `brew install --cask charlontank/tap/agents-sleep-preventer`.
- Windows x64: `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.8.6-windows-x86_64.zip`. Extract the full ZIP and launch `asp.exe`.
- Linux x86_64 (server mode): `AgentsSleepPreventer-5.8.6-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`. Check it against the SHA-256 shown here and on https://asp.charlon.dev/#install, or use https://asp.charlon.dev/install.sh, which checks it for you (read it first).
