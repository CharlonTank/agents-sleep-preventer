# Agents Sleep Preventer 5.2.0

## Sleep when done, with a time limit

- **Agents that never stop no longer keep the Mac up all night.** Some agents poll on a schedule, testing something every few minutes for hours, so "Sleep when done" never fired. Under "Sleep when done", pick when the Mac should sleep even if agents keep working: never, or in 30 min, 1 h, 1 h 30, 2 h or 2 h 30. If they finish earlier, the Mac sleeps then, as before.
- **Grant more time whenever you like.** Picking a duration counts from now, so choosing "in 2 h" again gives a long task two more hours. The status pill shows the time, e.g. "SLEEP BY 02:09".
- **Never under your hands.** Even past the limit, the Mac only sleeps once nobody has used it for a minute.
- **Stays asleep.** After a time-limit sleep, still-running agents no longer keep the Mac awake until someone uses it again.
- From the terminal: `asp force when-done --within 1h30`.
